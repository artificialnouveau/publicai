## Tentacular Fellowship 2026-2027 (Ocean Space, Venice)

An initiative-building fellowship for the Lagoon of Venice.

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Host | TBA21-Academy at Ocean Space, with NICHE Centre for Environmental Humanities and the UNESCO Chair on Water, Heritage and Sustainable Development at Ca' Foscari University, Venice |
| Duration | 15 months (September 2026 to November 2027) |
| Application deadline | July 5, 2026, 11:59pm CET |
| Total support | EUR 26,000 |
| Link | https://tba21.org/oceanspace-research-tentacular-fellowship |

### Funding breakdown

- Phase 1 (Incubation): EUR 3,000 grant to support independent research and initiative development.
- Phase 2 (Activation): EUR 8,000 grant to support independent research and initiative development, plus EUR 15,000 activation budget.

### What it is

Ocean Space is built as a long-term platform for artistic research and ecological engagement, aiming to move beyond awareness-raising toward real, tangible territorial effects through situated collaboration with the communities, practitioners, researchers, and civic actors already working in the Lagoon.

The fellowship invites a practitioner or collective to initiate, consolidate, or expand a situated initiative in the Lagoon of Venice through the creation of a Tentacular Unit: a collaborative interdisciplinary platform gathering artists, students, researchers, institutions, local communities, and other actors around a specific socio-environmental challenge. Through situated research, alliance-building, and public-facing formats, the fellow helps build the social, institutional, and ecological conditions for the initiative to take root and grow over time. The fellowship is based at Ocean Space and at Ca' Foscari in Venice and supports experimental, community-driven practices that activate regenerative processes with tangible ecosocial impact for the Lagoon.
